## Skills Covered
- Log ingestion and field extraction
- SPL filtering, aggregation, and time-based analysis
- Investigation of authentication and network activity
- Identification of unusual patterns for further investigation

## Recommended Investigation Format
For each completed investigation, document:
1. **Scenario and objective**
2. **Data source and lab setup**
3. **SPL query** and explanation of key fields
4. **Evidence** — sanitised sample events or screenshots
5. **Finding and analyst decision**
6. **False positives, limitations, and next steps**

## Tools
Splunk Enterprise/Free, SPL, and sample DNS, FTP, HTTP, SSH, SMTP, DHCP, or Zeek logs.

> Portfolio note: Follow the setup and results recorded in each individual exercise. Clearly distinguish tested findings from expected outcomes. Use only authorised lab data and do not claim production incident experience based on simulated exercises.
